Homework Tutor (tracked in the portfolio alongside its early working title AI Tutor) has finalized its visual identity around **Concept 3 — Smart Notebook** (*Розумний Зошит*).

## Visual identity & app icon

- **Notebook-and-pencil icon:** Homework Tutor uses its own dedicated blue rounded-square icon featuring a friendly spiral notebook page and yellow pencil. It does not use the HomeLabCore studio flask icon.
- **Smart Notebook UI direction:** Designed for students aged 9–16, the interface organizes study flows into a warm paper-toned home screen with clear entry points for photo and text tasks, subject cards, recent activity, and a spiral-bound navigation drawer.

## Current development status

Homework Tutor remains **in active development**:

- The Android prototype includes a working built-in sample tutoring flow (`3x + 7 = 22`) demonstrating step-by-step problem solving, Level 1 hints, and *Explain Differently* without immediately revealing the final answer.
- Live AI tutoring responses, camera task capture, and multi-subject production workflows are still under development and are not yet released to production.
