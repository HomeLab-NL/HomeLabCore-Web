CookFrom for Android is now live on Google Play (version 0.12.0), while the iOS edition is currently in testing.

## Verified changes

- Android: Released to Google Play production (`0.12.0`, package `com.homelabnl.recipeai`) with AI-assisted recipe generation from ingredients or photos, pantry management, shopping lists, and step-by-step Cooking Mode.
- Ingredient substitution: Context-aware local swaps, validated AI alternatives with strict allergy and dietary safety checks, and manual catalog replacement across 8 languages.
- iOS: Currently in testing via TestFlight MVP on the shared `0.12.0` codebase; public App Store release is not yet live.
- Links: [Get CookFrom on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.homelabnl.recipeai) and visit the [CookFrom product page](/apps/cookfrom).
