CookFrom 0.12.1 is now released for Android on Google Play and lets you decide where the rest of a recipe's ingredients come from, so pantry recipes stay within what you actually have at home.

## Verified changes

- How shall we cook: after picking ingredients, choose your Pantry, only the products you picked, or a free chef's idea.
- Pantry recipes respect what is in stock, with an optional allowance of up to 2 missing items that are clearly marked in the recipe.
- Recipes that cannot be made from the chosen ingredients are now declined with a clear message instead of serving an incomplete dish.
- A Back arrow is available in every search, including the product picker.
- Steamer added to cooking equipment.
- Built-in recipes now include chef's tips and step timers in all 8 languages.
- Android: version 0.12.1 (build 145) released on Google Play.
- iOS: available for testing through TestFlight; not yet released on the App Store.
