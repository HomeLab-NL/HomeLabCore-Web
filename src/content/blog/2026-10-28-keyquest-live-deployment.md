KeyQuest (originally prototyped under the working title Keyboard Trainer) is now deployed as a live browser application at [keyquest-b9l7.onrender.com](https://keyquest-b9l7.onrender.com/).

## What is included in the live build

- **Typing Academy (10 lessons):** A structured curriculum progressing from Home Row Left (`A S D F`) and Home Row Right (`J K L ;`) through full Home Row words, Top Row (`Q W E R T Y U I O P`), Bottom Row (`Z X C V B N M`), Shift capitals, numbers, punctuation, speed practice, and the Final Exam. Each lesson displays an interactive color-coded keyboard with finger-zone indicators and live WPM/accuracy tracking.
- **Word Rush:** A 60-second timed wave survival mode with escalating word waves, streak combo multipliers, and a three-heart life counter.
- **Starwood Trail (Typing Adventure):** A five-stage story quest across Sunny Forest, Crystal Cave, Misty Bridge, Fire Mountain, and Typo Castle against King Typo. Each completed word powers a hero attack before the monster attack timer fills.
- **Progression & Trophy Shelf:** Players earn 1–3 stars per lesson based on accuracy and WPM targets, gain XP across all modes, and unlock 12 Trophy Shelf achievements.
- **Local storage & Web Audio:** All progress is stored locally in the browser (`localStorage`) with no sign-up or external tracking, paired with synthesized Web Audio sound effects and a header sound toggle.

## Hackathon context

KeyQuest was developed with participation in the Stripe Eindhoven Community hackathon, associated with One Horizon.
