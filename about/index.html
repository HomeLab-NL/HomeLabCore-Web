About HomeLabCore

HomeLabCore is a small, independent software studio. It designs, builds, and maintains a short list of focused apps rather than a large catalogue.

The aim is straightforward: make practical software for everyday problems, keep it maintained, and be clear about how it handles your data. Every app has its own product page, privacy policy, and support page.

HomeLabCore is the name these apps are published under. Questions are welcome at privacy@homelabcore.dev or support@homelabcore.dev.
