Ops Planner

Plan your team's week.

Ops Planner helps you share out a department's tasks, duties, and events among employees, day by day. Drag a task onto a person, see everyone's workload at a glance, and send each person their schedule.

How it works

From a list of work to a weekly plan

1. Add your team

Enter employees with their position, department, working days and hours, email address, and vacation periods.

2. Build the pool

Create the recurring work of your department as tasks, duties, and events, with estimated hours and times.

3. Assign and share

Drag items onto employees for a day or the whole week, then email each person their schedule or send a workload report to the manager.

Features

What Ops Planner includes

Drag-and-drop weekly planning

Assign a task, duty, or event to an employee for one day, the whole working week, or any set of days you pick. The same item can go to several people.

Workload at a glance

Each employee shows a workload percentage calculated from assigned hours and their own working schedule, so over- and under-loaded people stand out.

Employees, departments & schedules

Keep employees grouped by department, with individual working days, hours, and breaks.

Vacations & public holidays

Record vacation periods — the planner won't assign work to someone who is away. Public holidays for Ukraine or the Netherlands are marked on the calendar.

Task status & transfer

Mark assigned tasks as done or in progress, and transfer an assignment to another employee for one day or the whole week.

Share schedules by email

Send an employee their schedule for a day or the week. Ops Planner prepares the message and opens it in your own email app for you to review and send.

Excel workload report

Generate an .xlsx report of assignments and workload for the selected days and attach it to an email to the manager, again through your own email app.

Export & import

Save a full copy of your data to a JSON file wherever you choose, and restore it later — for example when moving to a new phone.

Local-first

Your plan stays on your phone

No account. No server.

Everything you enter in Ops Planner is stored only on your device. There is no sign-up, no cloud sync, no advertising, and no analytics. Data leaves the device only when you choose to send an email or export a file.

  • No account needed
  • No ads or tracking
