How it works

From ingredients to a recipe

1. Choose ingredients

Select the ingredients you already have, or point your camera at them and let CookFrom recognize ingredients from a photo.

2. Generate a recipe

CookFrom uses AI-assisted recipe generation to suggest something you can make, taking your dietary preferences and allergies into account.

3. Cook and adjust

Adjust servings, edit the recipe, and switch to Cooking Mode to follow the steps while you work.

Features

What CookFrom includes

Ingredients & photo recognition

Choose ingredients from a list, or recognize ingredients from a photo to build your selection faster.

AI-assisted recipe generation

Turn your selected ingredients into a recipe suggestion, then regenerate or refine it.

Pantry & inventory

Keep track of what is in your kitchen so recipe suggestions match what you actually have.

Shopping list

Collect the items you still need into a shopping list you can take to the store.

Favorites & recipe history

Save recipes you like and look back at ones you generated before.

Dietary preferences & allergies

Set dietary preferences, allergies, and dislikes so recipes are drafted with them in mind.

Servings adjustment & recipe editing

Scale a recipe up or down for the number of people you are cooking for, and edit the details to suit you.

Cooking Mode

Follow a recipe step by step in a view designed for use while you cook.
